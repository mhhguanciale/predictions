Macroeconomic & Fixed Income Strategy

Stagflationary Pressures Solidifying

  • Inflation Floor: Core CPI continues its steady upward march (reaching 333.5 in February), and markets are overwhelmingly pricing in headline inflation remaining above 3% for the entirety of 2026.
  • Labor Market Softening: Unemployment has ticked up to 4.4%, and job openings (JOLTS) have trended downward to 6.88M. Combined with depressed consumer sentiment (56.6), the macro environment is heavily skewed toward stagflation.
  • Rate Path: Do not expect a Fed rate cut in April. The market is near-certain the Fed will hold the funds rate at 3.64%. Expect a "higher for longer" regime to combat oil-driven inflation.

Fixed Income & Credit Risks

  • Yield Curve Dynamics: The 10Y Treasury yield has climbed back to 4.33%, un-inverting the curve (10Y-2Y spread is +0.52%). Markets heavily favor the 10Y breaching 4.5% this year. Short-duration Treasuries remain the safest yield play.
  • Private Credit Contagion: Blue Owl’s withdrawal limits signal acute stress in private credit markets. Highly levered middle-market companies will struggle with sustained 4%+ long-term rates and rising energy input costs. Avoid exposure to direct lending vehicles and highly levered small-caps.
  • Sovereign Debt: The US national debt is virtually guaranteed to cross $40 trillion this year. This structural deficit will continue to put upward pressure on term premiums.

Geopolitics & Commodities

Middle East Conflict & Energy

  • Oil Spike: WTI crude surged to $104.69 at the end of March. With direct US military strikes on Iranian infrastructure and retaliatory strikes on the Gulf, the risk premium on oil is permanent for the near term. Markets strongly favor crude settling above $84 through the summer.
  • Second-Order Effects: Overweight domestic exploration and production equities. Short airlines and logistics companies that lack fuel surcharge pricing power (unlike Amazon).

Defense & Global Security

  • Domestic Defense: Trump's firing of the Army Chief of Staff and threats to withdraw from NATO introduce political volatility into the defense sector. However, the active kinetic war ensures elevated defense spending.
  • Focus on US Primes: Favor US-centric defense contractors and autonomous/AI defense tech (e.g., Palantir, Anduril if it IPOs) over European-exposed legacy primes, given the NATO friction.

Sector & Equity Allocation

Technology & AI

  • Mega-Cap Resilience: Microsoft and Google's rapid deployment of new models (Gemma 4) shows the AI capex cycle is unbroken. These mega-caps serve as defensive growth assets in a stagflationary environment.
  • OpenAI IPO: Markets are heavily pricing an OpenAI IPO this year with a valuation exceeding $800 billion. This will be a massive liquidity event; expect a rotation out of secondary software names to fund allocations into OpenAI.

Space & Aerospace

  • The SpaceX Vacuum: SpaceX targeting a $2 trillion IPO valuation will fundamentally re-rate the aerospace sector. Prepare for a massive liquidity suck from broader tech and legacy aerospace (Boeing, Lockheed) into SpaceX.

Healthcare & Pharmaceuticals

  • Tariff Fallout: Trump's 100% tariff on patented drugs will crush margins for multinational pharma companies reliant on imported IP and manufacturing. Pivot aggressively toward domestic generic manufacturers and US-based biotech.
  • Pediatric Health: The spread of the 'Cicada' COVID variant and regional measles outbreaks provide a short-term catalyst for vaccine manufacturers and pediatric healthcare facilities.

Consumer Discretionary

  • Margin Squeeze: Amazon's 3.5% surcharge is a leading indicator. Retailers without the market power to pass on fuel costs will see Q2 margins compress.
  • Government Shutdown: The ongoing DHS funding standoff will further depress consumer spending in government-heavy regions. Underweight broad consumer discretionary.

Alternatives

  • Gold: Remains a premier hedge. The combination of kinetic war, un-anchored US debt, and sticky inflation makes gold highly attractive.
  • Bitcoin: Markets are pricing a strong likelihood of BTC crossing $80k this year. It continues to trade as a high-beta liquidity sponge, but be wary of short-term drawdowns if the Fed signals unexpected hawkishness due to oil prices.
