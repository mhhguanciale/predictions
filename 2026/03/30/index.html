Macroeconomic Outlook & Fixed Income

  • Stagflationary Pressures: Recent data prints show unemployment ticking up to 4.4% and retail sales stagnating, while Core CPI and PCE continue to grind higher. Market pricing indicates a near-certainty that annual inflation will remain stubbornly above 3% for the duration of 2026, exacerbated by surging energy costs.
  • Fed Policy: With the effective Fed Funds rate currently at 3.64%, the market expects a hawkish pivot. Expect a "Pause-Pause-Pause" trajectory over the next three FOMC meetings. The prospect of zero to one rate cut for the entirety of 2026 is now the base case.
  • Yield Curve & Duration: The 10-year Treasury yield (currently 4.42%) faces severe upward pressure from sticky inflation and deficit spending. Expect the 10-year to breach 4.5% and likely test 4.6% in the near term. The yield curve has un-inverted (10Y-2Y spread at +46 bps); maintain a defensive posture by underweighting long-duration bonds and favoring short-term paper or TIPS.
  • Fiscal & Political Risk: A divided government is highly likely post-midterms, with strong consensus pointing to the House flipping. This will complicate upcoming debt ceiling negotiations (X-date approaching in May/June), injecting volatility into short-term T-bills.

Geopolitics & Trade

  • Middle East Escalation: A direct US/Israel conflict with Iran is the dominant market driver. Expect imminent military action targeting Iranian oil infrastructure (specifically Kharg Island and processing facilities). This will severely disrupt Strait of Hormuz shipping lanes.
  • Aggressive Trade Policy: A 10% blanket US import tariff is effectively priced in for the immediate future, alongside severe risks of a 100% tariff on Canadian imports. Prepare for immediate supply chain disruptions, retaliatory FX volatility, and margin compression for import-heavy sectors.
  • Russia/Ukraine: Ceasefire prospects remain bleak for 2026. The conflict is expected to grind on, maintaining a floor under global agricultural and European energy prices.

Sector Allocation

  • Energy (Strong Overweight): WTI crude recently spiked to nearly $99/bbl and is expected to easily breach $100/bbl in April due to Middle East supply shocks. Heavily overweight E&P, oil services, and domestic natural gas.
  • Defense & Aerospace (Overweight): Direct beneficiaries of the US troop deployments and active Middle East ground operations. Furthermore, the highly anticipated SpaceX IPO (expected to debut at a $1T+ valuation) will serve as a massive sentiment catalyst for the broader aerospace supply chain.
  • Technology & AI (Market Weight / Selective): The AI arms race remains the primary structural growth engine. OpenAI's GPT-5.5/6 releases are expected by mid-year, and an OpenAI IPO is heavily anticipated. However, limit exposure to hardware manufacturers vulnerable to US-China trade tensions and impending AI-chip export licensing laws.
  • Consumer Discretionary (Underweight): Consumer sentiment remains depressed (currently 56.6). The combination of $100+ oil, rising unemployment, and sticky inflation will severely compress discretionary spending and retail margins.

Alternatives

  • Gold: Safe-haven demand is surging due to the confluence of Middle East conflict, sticky inflation, and fiat currency volatility. Expect gold to maintain strong support well above current levels.
